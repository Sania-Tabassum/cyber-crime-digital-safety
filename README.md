# Cyber Crime & Digital Safety

## About the Project
An academic project focused on understanding cyber crime, digital risks, and safe practices for using digital platforms.

## Objective
To study common cyber threats and understand how individuals can protect their personal information and maintain digital safety.

## Work Done
- Researched common forms of cyber crime
- Studied digital security risks and prevention measures
- Collected and organized relevant information
- Prepared structured findings for awareness and prevention

## Key Learning
Developed awareness of digital risks, responsible online practices, and the importance of protecting personal information.

## Skills Demonstrated
Research | Information Analysis | Documentation | Presentation | Attention to Detail
